# VCDC Ride Leader App — Training Video Scripts

Two complete scripts for a short training video, built as **8-second segments** you can
string together. Same seven lessons in both; different structure, tone, and shooting effort.

- **Version A — "Two Minutes, Seven Things"** — direct walkthrough. Screen recording plus
  voiceover. Can be finished in an afternoon. This is the one to make first.
- **Version B — "Una Domenica" (One Sunday)** — narrative. Follows a leader through an
  actual ride morning. Needs b-roll of real scooters and a real parking lot. Better film,
  more work.

Both run **15 segments ≈ 2:00**.

---

## Before you shoot: three things

**1. One feature in these scripts does not exist yet.**

The **closing comment** — the leader's note written after the ride is submitted — is
scoped but not built. It is segment **A14** and **B14**. Either build it before filming,
or cut that segment and use the alternate line provided in each script.

The **pre-ride note** *does* exist today: it is the **Notes** field on the ride creation
form, and it displays on the ride page. Segments A6 and B5 use the real thing.

**2. Do not film real member data.**

Every card on screen shows a real person's name and member number. Create one test member
("Demo Rider", a number outside the real range) and film only that card. Same for the
rides list — make a demo ride, not a real one.

**3. Say exactly what the buttons say.**

The voiceover should use the app's own words so people can follow along on their phone.
The glossary at the bottom of this file lists every label verbatim. If the app text and
the script disagree, the app wins — change the script.

---

## Tone guardrails

The club is Italian-adjacent and everyone in it rides a Vespa. That earns a light touch,
not a costume.

**Yes:** espresso on a kitchen table, Sunday morning light, the sound of a two-stroke
warming up, a handful of words used naturally — *allora, ecco, piano piano, andiamo,
basta*. Warmth. Unhurried. The voice of somebody who has led this ride before.

**No:** exaggerated accents, *mamma mia*, mandolin, pizza, checkered flags, tarantella,
anything a tourist shop would sell. If a line would make an actual Italian wince, cut it.

**Narrator:** one voice, warm, mid-pace, faintly accented at most. Somebody in the club is
better than a hired read.

**Pace:** 8 seconds is roughly **20 to 24 words**. Every line below is inside that. If you
rewrite one, count it.

# VERSION A — "Two Minutes, Seven Things"

Straight walkthrough. Screen recording with a hand holding the phone where it helps.
Warm and quick, no story. This is the version that will actually get watched twice.

**Runtime:** 15 × 8s = 2:00
**Music:** light acoustic, Italian café, low under the whole thing. Duck for voiceover.

---

### A1 — Cold open (0:00–0:08)

**On screen:** Close on a clipboard with a curling sign-in sheet, a pen tied to it with
string. A gloved hand tries the pen. Nothing. Cut to black, then a phone lighting up.

**Voiceover:**
> "Sunday morning. Twenty riders, one clipboard, and a pen that does not work. Allora — let us try something better."

**Text on screen:** *(none — let the pen do the work)*

---

### A2 — What this is (0:08–0:16)

**On screen:** The rides list on a phone, clean and quiet. A finger scrolls once.

**Voiceover:**
> "This is the VCDC Ride Leader app. Seven things, two minutes. By the end you can run a whole ride from your phone."

**Text on screen:** **VCDC Ride Leader App** / *Seven things. Two minutes.*

---

### A3 — Logging in (0:16–0:24)

**On screen:** The login screen. Typing an email. Cursor moves to the password box — and
deliberately skips it. Tap the button.

**Voiceover:**
> "Type your club email. Leave the password blank — that box is only for admins. Then tap the button."

**Text on screen:** **Email me a sign-in link** *(match the button exactly)*

---

### A4 — Your sign-in link (0:24–0:32)

**On screen:** Email inbox on the same phone. The message arrives. Tap the link. The rides
list opens.

**Voiceover:**
> "Check your email on the phone you will actually ride with. Tap the link, and you are in. No password to forget."

**Text on screen:** *No password. Just the link.*

---

### A5 — Your own card link (0:32–0:40)

**On screen:** The member card page with its QR. Then the share sheet → Add to Home
Screen. The icon lands on the home screen next to the app.

**Voiceover:**
> "Your member card has its own link, sent to you by the club. Save it to your home screen. That QR code is you."

**Text on screen:** *Your card link → home screen*

---

### A6 — Creating a ride (0:40–0:48)

**On screen:** Tap **New ride**. Fill the form, field by field: Ride name, Date, Meeting
point, Notes. Tap **Create ride**.

**Voiceover:**
> "New ride. Name it, date it, add the meeting point. The notes box is where you tell the group what today looks like."

**Text on screen:** **Ride name · Date · Meeting point · Notes**

> **Note:** this is the "comment before the ride" beat. The Notes field is real and shows
> on the ride page for anyone who opens it.

---

### A7 — The one that matters (0:48–0:56)

**On screen:** The yellow **Before you leave the house** panel on the ride page. Tap
**Open sign-in**. The chip flips to "94 on this phone". Then — deliberately — airplane
mode goes on and the roster chip stays.

**Voiceover:**
> "Now the important one. Open sign-in while you still have signal. That copies the roster onto your phone. Do it before you leave."

**Text on screen:** ⚠️ **Open sign-in at home, on wifi**

> **Direction:** slow this one down. It is the single most common way a ride goes wrong.
> Hold the shot of the roster chip surviving airplane mode for a full beat.

---

### A8 — The first scan (0:56–1:04)

**On screen:** Parking lot. **Start camera**. Point at a card. Green flash, and the result
card fills the screen: name, member number, membership through a date.

**Voiceover:**
> "In the lot: start camera, point at the card. Green means checked in. Name, number, membership — right there."

**Text on screen:** ✅ **Checked in**

---

### A9 — Reading the waiver line (1:04–1:12)

**On screen:** Three result cards in quick succession, each held ~2.5s. Green "Waiver
signed" with a date. Green "Covered by membership." Red "No waiver on file."

**Voiceover:**
> "Under the name is the waiver line. Signed, with a date. Covered by membership. Or red — no waiver on file."

**Text on screen:** *Signed · Covered · Missing*

---

### A10 — Signing on the spot (1:12–1:20)

**On screen:** Red card. Tap **Sign waiver now**. The waiver dialog opens. Phone passes to
a second pair of hands. They type a name, tick the box, tap **Sign waiver**. Green
confirmation. Phone comes back.

**Voiceover:**
> "Red is not a problem, it is a prompt. Tap Sign waiver now and hand them the phone. Name, tick, done."

**Text on screen:** **Sign waiver now** → *Waiver signed*

---

### A11 — Somebody with no card (1:20–1:28)

**On screen:** Tap **Show waiver code**. The QR fills the screen. The phone turns around
to face a second rider, who scans it with their own phone. Cut to them showing their new
guest QR back, and it scanning green as **Guest**.

**Voiceover:**
> "Somebody brought a friend? Tap Show waiver code and turn the phone around. They sign on theirs, then you scan what comes back."

**Text on screen:** **Show waiver code** → *they sign → you scan*

---

### A12 — Doubles and dead spots (1:28–1:36)

**On screen:** Same card scanned twice — the amber **Already on the list** badge appears.
Then the status chips: **No signal**, and **3 waiting to send**. Signal returns, the
count drops to zero on its own.

**Voiceover:**
> "Scan the same person twice and it says already on the list. No bars? It keeps working, and everything sends when signal returns."

**Text on screen:** *Already on the list · 3 waiting to send*

---

### A13 — Closing the ride (1:36–1:44)

**On screen:** End of the ride, kickstands down. The **Submit 18 riders** button. Tap.
The green Submitted panel replaces the scanner.

**Voiceover:**
> "Kickstands up. Tap submit, and the roster goes in. Sign-in closes — a submitted ride does not change afterwards."

**Text on screen:** **Submit 18 riders**

---

### A14 — The closing note (1:44–1:52)

> ⚠️ **Not built yet.** Either build the closing comment before filming, or use the
> alternate below and drop to a 14-segment, 1:52 cut.

**On screen:** Café table, helmet off, espresso. Opening the finished ride and typing a
short comment into it.

**Voiceover:**
> "Then leave your closing note. How it went, who peeled off early, anything the next leader should know. One paragraph is plenty."

**Text on screen:** *Every ride ends with a note*

**Alternate (if the feature is not built):**
> "Every rider you scanned is now on the club's record, matched up and counted. Nothing to hand in, nothing to type up later."

---

### A15 — Where it all lives (1:52–2:00)

**On screen:** The rides list scrolling to the **Submitted** section. Open one — the
**Signed in (18)** list. Pull back to a wide shot of scooters pulling out.

**Voiceover:**
> "Every ride stays on your list with everyone who signed in. The safety guidance and club ride rules are linked there too. Andiamo."

**Text on screen:** **vcdc.club** *(or wherever this lives)* / *Questions? Ask in the group.*

# VERSION B — "Una Domenica"

One leader, one Sunday, kitchen table to espresso. The instruction rides along inside the
story. Needs real b-roll: scooters, a real meeting point, hands, morning light. Screen
recordings drop in as inserts rather than carrying the film.

**Runtime:** 15 × 8s = 2:00
**Music:** solo guitar or piano, sparse at the start, fuller from B7, quiet again at B14.
**Look:** warm, slightly overexposed morning light. Shoot the phone in hands, outdoors,
never as a floating rectangle.

---

### B1 — 6:40am (0:00–0:08)

**On screen:** A moka pot ticking on a stove. A helmet on a kitchen chair. A phone face
down on the table beside a set of keys.

**Voiceover:**
> "Six-forty on a Sunday. The espresso is ready before you are. Today you are leading — and the whole ride fits in your pocket."

**Sound:** moka pot, a distant scooter passing.

---

### B2 — The link (0:08–0:16)

**On screen:** Phone picked up. Login screen, email typed, password box skipped. Tap.
Inbox. The link. In.

**Voiceover:**
> "First the app. One email, one link, no password. Tap it on the phone that is coming with you today."

**Text on screen:** **Email me a sign-in link**

---

### B3 — Your own card (0:16–0:24)

**On screen:** The member card page, QR bright on the screen. Thumb saves it to the home
screen.

**Voiceover:**
> "Your own card is already there, at its own link. That QR is how the next leader scans you in. Save it to your home screen."

---

### B4 — Two minutes at the table (0:24–0:32)

**On screen:** Over the shoulder at the kitchen table. **New ride**. Ride name typed —
*Rock Creek Loop*. Date. Meeting point — *Meridian Hill Park*. **Create ride**.

**Voiceover:**
> "Kitchen table, two minutes. Ride name. Date. Meeting point. Create. The ride exists, and so does your morning."

**Text on screen:** **New ride**

---

### B5 — A line for the group (0:32–0:40)

**On screen:** Close on the **Notes** field. Typing: *Coffee stop at mile 20. Easy pace.
Gravel on the last half mile — take it slow.*

**Voiceover:**
> "In the notes, a line for the group. Where you stop for coffee, the pace, the road that is still gravel."

**Text on screen:** *Notes → everyone sees this*

---

### B6 — Before the helmet (0:40–0:48)

**On screen:** Still at home, wifi bars full. Tapping **Open sign-in**. The chip reads
**94 on this phone**. Then the phone goes in the jacket pocket and the helmet goes on.

**Voiceover:**
> "Before the helmet goes on — open sign-in here, on good wifi. The roster copies onto the phone. That is what saves you later."

**Text on screen:** ⚠️ **Do this at home**

> **Direction:** this is the beat that prevents the most support calls. Give the roster
> chip a clean, held shot.

---

### B7 — Ten to nine (0:48–0:56)

**On screen:** Wide, the meeting point. Scooters arriving in ones and twos. Helmets off,
handshakes. The leader standing with the phone already out.

**Voiceover:**
> "Meridian Hill, ten to nine. They arrive in ones and twos — and every one of them needs to be on your list."

**Sound:** engines, voices, no music for two seconds.

---

### B8 — One second per rider (0:56–1:04)

**On screen:** Tight on the phone. **Start camera**. A card comes up. Green flash. Next
rider already stepping forward. Repeat, fast, three times.

**Voiceover:**
> "Start camera. Point at the card. Green. Name, number, membership — one second per rider, and the queue keeps moving."

**Text on screen:** ✅ **Checked in**

---

### B9 — The line under the name (1:04–1:12)

**On screen:** Three result cards, held. Green with a date. Green, covered. Then red.

**Voiceover:**
> "Read the line under the name. Signed with a date. Covered by membership. Or red — which just means today is the day."

**Text on screen:** *Signed · Covered · No waiver on file*

---

### B10 — Pass them the phone (1:12–1:20)

**On screen:** **Sign waiver now**. The phone changes hands. A rider, still in gloves,
pulls one off to type. Ticks the box. Hands it back. Green.

**Voiceover:**
> "Tap Sign waiver now and pass them the phone. They type their name, tick the box, and hand it back. Ecco."

**Text on screen:** **Sign waiver now**

---

### B11 — The friend who came along (1:20–1:28)

**On screen:** A rider with no card, slightly sheepish. **Show waiver code**. The phone

**Voiceover:**
> "Somebody brought a friend with no card. Show waiver code, turn the phone round. They sign on theirs — you scan what comes back."

**Text on screen:** **Show waiver code**

### B12 — No bars (1:28–1:36)

**On screen:** A gravel lot under trees. The status chip reads **No signal**. Scanning

**Voiceover:**
> "Gravel lot, no bars, no problem. The roster is already on the phone. Keep scanning — it all sends when you are back."

**Text on screen:** *No signal · 4 waiting to send*

**On screen:** End of the ride. Scooters in a row. **Submit 18 riders**. The tap. The

## Three things worth repeating on screen

If the video only lands three points, make them these.

1. **Open sign-in at home, on wifi.** The roster copies to the phone there and nowhere
   else. Everything offline depends on it.
2. **Red is a prompt, not a problem.** A missing waiver is thirty seconds of typing, not a
   reason to hold up the ride.
3. **Nobody gets left off.** Duplicates, dead spots, a backgrounded phone — the list lives
   on the phone first and syncs when it can.
